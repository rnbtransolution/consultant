# TikTok Promo Video: R&B SOP Standard

## Goal

Create one concise, export-ready TikTok video promoting R&B SOP Standard, using the supplied website as the factual and visual source. The primary audience is hospitality business owners and operators across sectors: hotels, restaurants, cafes, wellness, and related service businesses.

## Audience, message, and action

- **Audience:** Hospitality business owners and operators across sectors.
- **Core message:** Tailored, practical SOPs and team training help service businesses make daily operations more consistent and prepare to scale.
- **Primary action:** Invite viewers to book a free consultation through the R&B Transolution website/LINE contact.
- **Claims:** Do not add unsupported outcome statistics. The website's "70% Faster Staff Training" claim will be omitted unless the owner separately confirms its substantiation and requests its use.

## Creative direction

- **Format:** 9:16 vertical, 1080 × 1920, approximately 25–30 seconds, suitable for TikTok.
- **Style:** Premium editorial animation aligned with the website's deep forest green, champagne-gold accents, refined serif headings, and hospitality imagery.
- **Visual language:** Animate existing product visuals (`assets/sop_suite_mockup.jpg` and `assets/sop_flowchart_mockup.jpg`) with restrained parallax, document/card reveals, checklist ticks, workflow-line motion, and clean typography. Avoid relying on new unlicensed stock imagery.
- **Language:** Thai narration with concise bilingual Thai/English on-screen text.
- **Sound:** A tasteful instrumental-only music bed (for example, soft piano/plucked strings with light percussion), sourced from a properly licensed track or composed for this project. No lyrics. Keep narration clear above the music and provide a clean ending.
- **Deliverables:** A single final MP4 in the Website project directory. Do not modify `index.html`, add a website embed, or deploy/publish the video as part of this work.

## Production and quality constraints

- Preserve the supplied logo/brand identity and maintain readable contrast and safe margins for TikTok's interface overlays.
- Use accurate Thai spelling, short bilingual captions, and pacing that remains readable on a phone.
- Use only assets belonging to the website or media with a clear license; document soundtrack source/license alongside the final asset.
- Do not fabricate client endorsements, quantified savings/results, or product functionality not stated on the site.
- Review the rendered MP4 for audio/video sync, clipping, caption safe areas, and correct final duration before calling it complete.

## Scope and acceptance

In scope: storyboard-based animated promotional MP4 and its instrumental soundtrack/narration mix.

Out of scope: edits to website source files, HTML video embedding, deployment to TikTok, ad campaign setup, and production of multiple versions.

Acceptance criteria:

1. Final MP4 is 1080 × 1920 vertical and 25–30 seconds long.
2. Thai voiceover and concise bilingual on-screen text match the approved story and call to action.
3. Instrumental-only music is audible but does not obscure narration, and its licensing/source is recorded.
4. Video uses the approved brand styling and source assets, with no unsupported performance claims.
5. The rendered file is inspected for completeness, legibility, timing, and audio sync; website files remain unchanged.
